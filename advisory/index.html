Our business boundaries

Clear about
our role.

AGF Advisory provides marketing, research, consulting and business development services. It does not hold customer funds, execute financial transactions or operate as a financial institution.

Our fintech and stablecoin work concerns markets, customers and commercial strategy. Any regulated activity remains with the client and its appropriately authorised providers.
