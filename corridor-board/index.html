Questions people ask

What exchange rate does this board show?

The mid-market reference rate — the midpoint between global buy and sell prices, before any provider adds a margin. It refreshes automatically every day from open.er-api.com. The apps listed will each offer slightly less than mid-market; the gap is part of what you pay.

Why is the rate in my app different from the rate here?

Money-transfer apps make part of their revenue on the FX margin: they buy at mid-market and sell to you a little below it. Comparing an app’s offer against the mid-market rate on this page shows you the real all-in cost, not just the visible fee.

Which countries and currencies are covered?

Six home countries — Kenya (KES), Nigeria (NGN), Ghana (GHS), Uganda (UGX), Rwanda (RWF) and South Africa (ZAR) — sending from Europe (EUR), USA (USD), Australia (AUD) and Asia (USD reference). Twenty-four corridors in total.

How does AGF pick the best app per corridor?

Picks come from Africa Global Forum’s own published research — the Remittance Apps 2026 YTD report, the Currency & Remittance Tracker, and Best Apps by Region — weighing fees, speed, corridor coverage and public app-store ratings. AGF has no commercial arrangement with any app on this board.
