Africa Global Forum · Our commitments

Independent thinking.
Clear relationships.

AGF brings together media, insights and commercial advisory. Readers should always know whose interests a piece of work serves.

Published 26 November 2026

Three divisions, distinct roles

AGF Media develops expert interviews, podcasts and educational videos. AGF Insights publishes research and market intelligence, and accepts commissioned research briefs. AGF Advisory provides commercial marketing, research, consulting and business development services.

The existing community and public report library remain part of AGF.

Editorial decisions stay with AGF

Payments for advisory, research or sponsorship do not buy favourable coverage, rankings, conclusions or interview selection. AGF retains editorial judgement over its independent publications, including which questions to investigate and how to present the evidence.

Commercial clients and sponsors may flag factual errors. They do not have approval or veto rights over independent editorial work.

Readers can see who funded the work

Publicly commissioned research is labelled “Commissioned research” and identifies the commissioning organisation. Sponsored media or content is labelled “Sponsored” and identifies the sponsor. Any material relationship relevant to the subject is disclosed alongside the content.

Independent editorial work, sponsored content and client deliverables are kept distinct in their presentation. A public commission is not presented as independent research.

Private client work remains separate

Commercial engagements have an agreed scope, deliverables and confidentiality terms. Confidential client research is not placed in the independent report library or used as undisclosed evidence in editorial work.

Access to AGF’s community does not include access to members’ private details. Community participation, introductions and any sharing of contact information require appropriate permission.

Experience is described accurately

Founder experience, current client work, sponsorship and formal partnerships are different relationships. We identify them as such and do not imply endorsements or outcomes without evidence and permission.

Questions, disclosures and corrections

If a commercial relationship needs clearer disclosure, or you believe a publication contains an error, email admin@africaglobalforum.com with a link and the issue. AGF will assess the evidence and update the relevant publication or disclosure where needed.

This policy applies to new work and updates from its publication date. It is a statement of AGF’s standards, not a claim that every historical publication has been independently audited.
